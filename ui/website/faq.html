Privacy & Data

When you use your own API key (e.g., your OpenAI key), your requests go directly to that provider under their own terms — check their terms if you want zero training opt-out. When you use our integrated access, we contractually prohibit training use.

Cryptographic erasure. We destroy your encryption key. Your stored data becomes mathematically unrecoverable — not "scheduled for deletion in 30 days" but immediately irreversible. This applies to all tiers.

Our privacy architecture was designed around these frameworks, not retrofitted to them.
