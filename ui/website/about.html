Why we exist

The AI industry made a deal you never agreed to: your conversations, your habits, your fears, your searches — all collected, analysed, and monetised. The product was never the AI. The product was you.

We think that's wrong. Not just ethically wrong — strategically wrong. AI companions are only valuable if you actually trust them. And you can't trust something that sells your secrets.

MEOK.AI starts from a different premise: you pay us for the service, so we work for you. Not for advertisers. Not for data brokers. Not for growth hackers optimising engagement metrics.

How most AI companies operate

  • Free tier funded by data collection
  • Conversations train future models (with your content)
  • Behavioral profiling for targeted ads
  • Dark patterns that keep you engaged, not well
  • Data stored on their servers, their keys
  • Deletion is a form — not actually deleted

How MEOK.AI operates

  • Subscription funded — you're the customer
  • Your conversations never train our models
  • No behavioral profiling, no ad targeting
  • Designed for your wellbeing, not your addiction
  • Local-first, client-side encryption
  • Cryptographic erasure — key destroyed, data gone

The egg

Our mascot is a hatching egg. It's deliberate. Your companion doesn't arrive fully formed — it grows with you. In the first 24 hours you get an egg. Over 48-72 hours, it cracks and hatches. Over weeks and months, it becomes something that genuinely knows you.

That's not a product feature. That's a philosophy. AI companions should earn your trust, not demand it on install.
