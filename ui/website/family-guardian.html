Our care-based promise

What Family Guardian actually does

📈

Builds a behavioural baseline

Learns what's normal for each child — their communication patterns, activity levels, social signals — so it can spot when something's genuinely different.

🚨

Alerts on anomalies, not everything

WhatsApp and SMS alerts only for significant anomalies. Not a firehose. Not every message. Just: "Something's different with your child this week."

📋

Weekly wellness reports

A gentle weekly summary — mood signals, social connection, any concerns. Built for conversation with your child, not confrontation.

👥

Up to 5 child profiles

Standard plan covers up to 5 children with individual baselines. Each child gets their own context — siblings are not compared to each other.
