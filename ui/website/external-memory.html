Memory that's actually yours

Every AI remembers things about you. The difference is who owns that memory. With MEOK, your memory archive is encrypted with your keys, stored on your device or optionally synced with zero-knowledge encryption. We can't read it. Neither can anyone you don't explicitly share with.

What makes this different from a note app

Companion-aware context

Your companion uses memory automatically — it doesn't wait for you to reference the past. Relevant context surfaces in every conversation.
