How it works

Three steps. Silent. Continuous. Precise.

Care Shield doesn't spam you with noise. It watches everything — and only speaks when something genuinely new and threatening appears.

1

Configure what to watch

Tell Care Shield your email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, domain names, and any other identifiers to protect. You control the scope entirely — add or remove targets any time. Profiles are encrypted at rest with your key.

2

Collectors run silently

Seven specialist OSINT collectors run continuously in the background — breach databases, dark web markets, paste sites, social threat vectors, domain spoofing registries, credential stuffing lists, and data broker listings. You don't need to do anything. They never sleep.

3

Alert only for new threats

Every finding is SHA-256 fingerprinted. If Care Shield has seen it before, it stays silent. You're only notified when something genuinely new surfaces — and only if it clears the severity threshold you've set. High-severity findings reach you via WhatsApp, push notification, and email simultaneously.

🔢

Differential fingerprinting — never alert twice

Each finding is hashed before storage. Care Shield compares incoming findings against its fingerprint ledger. If the hash already exists, the finding is discarded silently. You only hear about what's genuinely new.

SHA-256(source + entity + finding_content)
📲

Multi-channel alerts when it matters

High-severity findings trigger WhatsApp, push notification, and email — all three simultaneously, so you never miss a critical alert regardless of which channel you're on. Medium findings go to your dashboard only. Low findings are logged silently for your weekly digest.

Seven collectors. One shield.

Each collector is a specialist — purpose-built for its domain, running on its own schedule, feeding a unified threat ledger.

💥

Breach Database Collector

Monitors breach aggregators and raw dump sources for your email addresses, phone numbers, and usernames. Surfaces credential exposures before attackers can weaponise them.

🕸️

Dark Web Market Collector

Crawls known dark web markets and forums for listings referencing your identifiers — financial data, identity packages, access credentials sold to the highest bidder.

📋

Paste Site Collector

Monitors Pastebin, GitHub Gists, and dozens of public paste services for your email addresses, API keys, and personal identifiers accidentally leaked in code or data dumps.

🎭

Social Threat Collector

Detects impersonation accounts, coordinated harassment campaigns, and brand abuse across major social platforms. Alerts on accounts mimicking your identity or targeting you directly.

🌐

Domain Spoofing Collector

Watches newly registered domains for typosquats and homoglyph attacks targeting your domain — the kind used in phishing campaigns and CEO fraud that comes before the attack, not after.

🔑

Credential Stuffing Collector

Monitors known credential stuffing lists and combolist markets for your email-password pairs. If your credentials appear in an active stuffing list, you'll know immediately.

🗂️

Data Broker Collector

Tracks the data broker ecosystem — people-search sites, marketing databases, and aggregator profiles — showing you where your personal data is being bought and sold commercially.

Privacy by design — not a policy, an architecture

Watching the watchers doesn't mean becoming one. Care Shield is built so that monitoring external threats never requires holding your raw personal data internally.

🔒

No raw data stored

Identifiers are hashed before leaving your device. Care Shield queries against hashes — never plain-text emails or phone numbers.

🗑️

Cryptographic erasure

Delete your profile and your encryption key is destroyed — making stored fingerprints mathematically unreadable, permanently.

📊

Data minimisation

Care Shield stores only fingerprints and severity metadata. The raw content of a finding is never persisted beyond the alert delivery window.

🚫

No advertising. Ever.

Your threat profile is never used for targeting, modelling, or sold to third parties. Monitoring data exists for one purpose: protecting you.

🇪🇺

GDPR compliant

Full data portability, right to deletion, and audit logs available on request. Hosted in EU data centres by default.

🔍

Open audit logs

Every collector run, every alert fired, every fingerprint matched — logged and available to you in your dashboard at any time.
