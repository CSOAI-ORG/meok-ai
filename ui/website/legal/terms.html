2. AI disclosure and nature of the service

MEOK provides AI-powered companion services. This section exists because we believe transparency about AI is a foundational responsibility — and because it is required by the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).

2.1 What MEOK companions are

  • Software systems that generate responses using large language models (LLMs)
  • Companions designed to provide conversation, support, memory, and entertainment
  • AI systems subject to the limitations inherent in current LLM technology

2.2 What MEOK companions are not

  • Human beings — at no point are you speaking with a person unless explicitly stated
  • Omniscient or infallible — they make mistakes, can hallucinate, and may sometimes produce incorrect information
  • Replacements for qualified professionals — see Section 3
  • Capable of taking real-world actions on your behalf unless you have explicitly granted specific integrations

2.3 AI Act compliance

MEOK.AI companions are classified under the EU AI Act as general-purpose AI systems with a companion use case. We comply with applicable transparency obligations including this disclosure. We do not use high-risk AI system categories as defined by Annex III of the EU AI Act for consequential decisions about individuals.

2.4 How AI responses are generated

Your messages are processed by the LLM provider you have selected (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT, Google Gemini, or a local model). Responses reflect the training and capabilities of that model, shaped by MEOK's companion system and your memory context. Responses are not reviewed by humans before delivery.
