4. How long we keep it

We operate a tiered retention model. Less data, held for shorter periods, is always our default.

Tier 1

Ephemeral Processing

Real-time only. Emotional state indicators, in-session context signals. Processed and discarded within the session. Never written to disk. Never transmitted beyond the active session.

Tier 2

28-Day Quality & Safety

Anonymised interaction metrics and safety-monitoring signals. Retained for 28 days for quality assurance and abuse detection. Fully anonymised before storage — cannot be re-linked to you.

Tier 3

Long-Term Memory

Explicit opt-in required. Conversation history, saved memories, companion personality data. Retained until you delete it or cancel. Encrypted with your keys. You can delete at any time.

Account data retention

We retain account data (email, billing records) for the duration of your subscription plus 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes. Billing records are retained by our payment processor (Stripe) under their own data retention obligations.

After account deletion

When you delete your account:

  1. Immediate: Your data is removed from all production systems and active databases
  2. 30 days: Data is purged from encrypted backup archives
  3. Cryptographic verification: For Tier 3 data encrypted with your keys, deletion is verified by key destruction — the data is mathematically inaccessible regardless of physical storage
  4. We will provide a deletion confirmation email upon request

6. Data sovereignty and encryption

Data sovereignty is not a marketing claim at MEOK — it is an architectural constraint. The system is designed so that we cannot read your data without your keys, even if we wanted to.

6.1 Client-side encryption

All Tier 3 (long-term memory) data is encrypted on your device before it leaves your device, using AES-256-GCM. The encryption keys are derived from your credentials and stored in your device's secure enclave. MEOK servers receive and store only ciphertext — we have no ability to decrypt it.

6.2 Local-first architecture

By default, MEOK operates in local-first mode:

  • Your memory and companion data live on your device
  • The application functions fully offline
  • No data leaves your device unless you actively request it

6.3 Cloud sync

Cloud sync is opt-in only. If you enable sync:

  • Only encrypted ciphertext is uploaded — your keys are never uploaded
  • You can disable sync and delete cloud copies at any time
  • Sync data is stored in EU-based data centres by default (you can select region in Settings)

6.4 Key management

You are responsible for your encryption keys. If you lose access to your keys, we cannot recover your Tier 3 data. This is a feature, not a bug — it means no one (including us, including law enforcement without your cooperation) can access it. We provide secure key backup tools, but key custody remains yours.

Important: If you lose your encryption keys and have no backup, your long-term memory data cannot be recovered. Please use our built-in key backup feature (Settings > Security > Key Backup) to store an encrypted copy with a recovery passphrase.

7. Your rights

You have meaningful rights over your personal data. We take these seriously and have built the platform to make exercising them straightforward.

7.1 Right of access (Subject Access Request)

You can request a complete copy of all personal data we hold about you. We will respond within 30 days (14 days for CPRA requests). Data is provided in machine-readable format (JSON) plus human-readable HTML. Requests are free; we may charge a reasonable fee for manifestly excessive requests.

7.2 Right to rectification

If any data we hold is inaccurate, you can correct it directly in the app (Settings > Profile) or by contacting us.

7.3 Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")

You can request deletion of your personal data. Our tiered erasure process:

  • Immediate: Removed from all production systems and active caches
  • 30 days: Purged from encrypted backup archives (backup rotation cycle)
  • Cryptographic erasure: For client-encrypted Tier 3 data, key destruction renders data permanently inaccessible regardless of physical media
  • Exceptions: We may retain data required by law (e.g., billing records for 7 years under HMRC requirements) — we will tell you exactly what is retained and why

7.4 Right to portability

You can export all your data in structured, machine-readable format at any time. See Section 12 for full details.

7.5 Right to object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interest at any time. Upon a valid objection, we will cease that processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

7.6 Right to restriction

You can request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances — for example, while you contest its accuracy, or while an objection is being assessed.

7.7 Rights related to automated decision-making

MEOK does not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Care weight adjustments influence conversation tone, not consequential decisions about you.

7.8 How to exercise your rights

Most rights can be exercised directly in the app under Settings > Privacy & Data. You can also email privacy@meok.ai with "Data Rights Request" in the subject line. We will acknowledge within 3 business days and fulfil within the statutory timescale.

7.9 Right to complain

If you believe we have mishandled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority:

  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk
  • EU: Your national data protection authority (list at edpb.europa.eu)
  • California: California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA)
  • Canada: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

We would always prefer you contact us first so we can resolve any issue directly.

8. Children's privacy

MEOK takes the privacy and safety of children exceptionally seriously. We comply with COPPA (US), GDPR Article 8 (EU), and the UK Children's Code.

Age gate: Children under 13 require verifiable parental consent before accessing MEOK. Children aged 13–17 require parental acknowledgement. We do not knowingly collect data from children without appropriate consent.

8.1 Verifiable parental consent

For users under 13, we verify parental consent through a credit card micro-charge process (£0.01 charge, immediately refunded). This confirms an adult's involvement without storing unnecessary payment data. Alternative verification methods are available for families without credit cards — contact privacy@meok.ai.

8.2 Family Guardian data handling

  • Children's profile data is stored in a separate, independently encrypted data store from adult accounts
  • Children's data is encrypted with a separate key set controlled by the designated guardian
  • No behavioural profiling of minors under any circumstances
  • No advertising or marketing targeting based on children's data
  • AI companions for children are subject to enhanced content filtering and safety monitoring
  • Safety-critical conversations (e.g., self-harm indicators) trigger human escalation protocols

8.3 Guardian controls

Parents and guardians can, at any time:

  • Review all interaction logs for their child's companion (within the 28-day window)
  • Delete their child's data immediately
  • Withdraw consent and terminate the child's account
  • Adjust content filtering and session time limits
  • Export all data held about their child

8.4 If we discover underage access without consent

If we become aware that a child under 13 has accessed MEOK without verifiable parental consent, we will immediately suspend the account and delete all associated data within 72 hours. We will notify the account email to explain what happened and what was deleted.

9. Security and breach notification

9.1 Security measures

We implement the following technical and organisational measures:

  • AES-256-GCM encryption for all stored Tier 3 data
  • TLS 1.3 for all data in transit
  • Zero-knowledge architecture for client-encrypted data
  • Regular penetration testing (quarterly external audits)
  • SOC 2 Type II compliance (in progress; target Q3 2026)
  • Multi-factor authentication available and encouraged for all accounts
  • Employee access controls with principle of least privilege
  • Encrypted offsite backups with independent key management

9.2 Breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach:

  • Within 72 hours: We will notify the ICO (and relevant EU supervisory authorities) of any breach likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms
  • Without undue delay: We will notify affected individuals directly when the breach is likely to result in high risk to their rights and freedoms
  • Notifications will clearly describe: what happened, what data was affected, likely consequences, and steps we have taken or will take
  • We maintain an internal breach register regardless of whether notification is required
Note on client-encrypted data: A breach of our servers does not compromise your Tier 3 (long-term memory) data, because we hold only ciphertext. Even in a worst-case server compromise, your memories are mathematically protected.

10. International data transfers

MEOK AI LTD is based in England and Wales. Where we transfer personal data to countries outside the UK or EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Where required, we use the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs) or EU SCCs
  • Adequacy decisions: Where applicable (e.g., transfers covered by UK adequacy regulations)
  • Binding Corporate Rules: We are assessing BCR implementation for intragroup transfers

For transfers to LLM providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses supplemented by Transfer Impact Assessments.

You may request copies of the transfer mechanisms we rely on by emailing privacy@meok.ai.

11. Jurisdiction-specific rights

11.1 UK GDPR

This policy is drafted to comply with the UK GDPR and the UK Data Protection Act 2018. UK residents have all rights described in Section 7. Our lead supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

11.2 EU GDPR

EU residents have the same rights under the EU GDPR. We comply with GDPR as implemented in each EU member state. For EU residents, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses for data transfers to the UK.

11.3 California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • Right to know: What personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell (we do not sell)
  • Right to delete: Request deletion of your personal information (see Section 7.3)
  • Right to opt out of sale/sharing: We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising
  • Right to correct: Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: We process sensitive data only for the purposes you have consented to
  • Right to non-discrimination: Exercising your rights will not result in degraded service or different pricing

To exercise CCPA/CPRA rights, email privacy@meok.ai with "California Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days (extendable by a further 45 days with notice).

Do Not Sell or Share: We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11.4 Canada (PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25)

For Canadian users, we comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for Quebec residents, Law 25 (Law Modernizing Privacy Protection provisions).

Specific rights include:

  • Right to access personal information we hold about you
  • Right to challenge the accuracy and completeness of your information
  • Right to withdraw consent for non-essential processing
  • Quebec Law 25 additionally provides the right to data portability and the right to be de-indexed (where technically feasible)
  • Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs) are conducted for all new processing activities involving personal information

Our Privacy Officer for Canadian matters is reachable at privacy@meok.ai.

12. Data portability

Your data is yours. We provide comprehensive portability tools so you can take your data with you at any time, in an open format, without friction.

12.1 Export formats

  • JSON: Complete structured export of all account data, memories, and interaction history — machine-readable, importable to other services
  • HTML: Human-readable archive of conversations and memories, viewable in any browser without special software
  • Plain text: Simple .txt export of conversations for easy reading and archiving
  • Vector embeddings: Export of your memory embeddings in standard format for use with other AI tools

12.2 Real-time API access

Subscribers on Professional tier and above have API access to their own data in real time. Full documentation at docs.meok.ai/api.

12.3 Open-source tools

We publish open-source data migration tools under MIT licence at github.com/meok-ai, so you can verify how your data is processed and build your own migration workflows.

12.4 How to export

Settings > Privacy & Data > Export My Data. Full export is prepared within 24 hours and available for download for 7 days. You can request exports as often as you need.
