1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website or use a web-based application. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — for example, that you are logged in, what language you prefer, or that you have accepted certain settings.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies: Exist only while your browser is open. Deleted automatically when you close the browser.
  • Persistent cookies: Stored on your device for a defined period, or until you delete them. They persist between visits.
  • First-party cookies: Set by MEOK.AI directly.
  • Third-party cookies: Set by external services. See Section 6.

Beyond cookies, we may use similar technologies including local storage and session storage for the application to function. These are covered by the same consent framework described in this policy.

  • Google Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics. Google's advertising model is incompatible with our privacy commitments.

7. How we obtain and manage consent

7.1 Cookie consent banner

On your first visit to MEOK.AI, you will see a cookie consent banner offering:

  • Essential only: Accept only the cookies needed for the service to work (this is a legitimate, easy choice)
  • Accept functional: Enable preference-saving cookies for a more personalised experience
  • Accept analytics: Enable privacy-preserving analytics to help us improve the product
  • Accept all: Enable essential, functional, and analytics cookies
  • Manage preferences: Granular control over each category

7.2 Consent standards

Our consent process meets the requirements of:

  • UK PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003)
  • EU ePrivacy Directive (and the forthcoming ePrivacy Regulation)
  • GDPR and UK GDPR requirements for freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent
  • ICO guidance on cookies and consent (updated 2024)

7.3 No consent required for essential cookies

Essential cookies (Section 2) do not require consent. We set them based on the "strictly necessary" exemption in PECR Regulation 6(4). This is the law's recognition that some cookies are genuinely needed for the website to function.

7.4 Consent record

We record the fact that you gave or declined consent (timestamp, choices made) in the meok_prefs cookie. This record is used only to ensure we honour your choices and do not show you the consent banner unnecessarily.

8. Withdrawing consent

Withdrawing your consent is straightforward and has no negative effect on the core service.

8.1 How to withdraw

  • In the app: Settings > Privacy & Data > Cookie Preferences — you can change any non-essential consent at any time
  • On the website: Click "Cookie Settings" in the footer of any page
  • By email: privacy@meok.ai — we will process your withdrawal within 3 business days

8.2 Effect of withdrawal

  • Withdrawal takes effect immediately
  • Cookies you withdraw consent for are deleted from your device at the next page load
  • Withdrawing functional cookies means your language/theme/accessibility preferences will not persist between sessions — the service still works, you just lose personalisation
  • Withdrawing analytics consent means your usage is no longer included in our analytics data
  • There is no service degradation to core features from withdrawing non-essential cookie consent
Accessibility exception: If you rely on accessibility settings (meok_a11y) for functional reasons, withdrawing functional cookie consent may affect your ability to use the service comfortably. We recommend keeping functional cookies enabled if you use accessibility features. You can exempt specific cookies in Settings > Privacy & Data > Cookie Preferences.

10. Browser-level controls

In addition to our consent controls, you can manage cookies at the browser level. This gives you full control regardless of our settings.

10.1 How to manage cookies in your browser

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions
  • Brave: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data

10.2 Browser-level blocking

If you block all cookies at the browser level, MEOK.AI will not function correctly — you will not be able to log in (authentication requires the session cookie). We recommend using our in-app Cookie Preferences rather than blanket browser-level blocking, as our controls offer the same privacy outcomes without breaking the service.

10.3 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. We honour the spirit of DNT: we do not engage in cross-site tracking, and our analytics are privacy-preserving by design. We don't need a DNT signal to behave well — it's our default.

10.4 Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We support the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we treat it as equivalent to opting out of any non-essential data processing, consistent with CCPA/CPRA requirements for California users.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this Cookie Policy when we change the cookies we use or when legal requirements change. Significant changes will be communicated via:

  • Email notification at least 14 days before the change takes effect
  • An updated cookie consent banner asking for fresh consent where required
  • The "Last updated" date at the top of this page

Minor clarifications (e.g., updated cookie durations, descriptive improvements) may be made without notification, but will always be reflected in the "Last updated" date.
