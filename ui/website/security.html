Security & Responsible
Disclosure

MEOK.AI is built on a foundation of sovereignty and trust. We take security seriously — not as a compliance checkbox, but as an architectural commitment. If you've found a vulnerability, we want to hear from you. We operate a coordinated disclosure programme with real rewards and genuine safe harbour.

OWASP LLM Top 10 Compliance

We actively track and address the OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications. Below is our current compliance posture across all ten categories.

ID Category Description Status Controls
LLM01 Prompt Injection Crafted inputs manipulating LLM behaviour to bypass instructions or exfiltrate data. Active Input sanitisation, system prompt hardening, output filtering, jailbreak detection layer.
LLM02 Insecure Output Handling LLM output passed downstream without validation, enabling XSS, SSRF, or code execution. Active Output encoding at render boundaries, content-type enforcement, CSP headers on all responses.
LLM03 Training Data Poisoning Malicious data injected into training or fine-tuning pipelines to alter model behaviour. Planned Provenance tracking for fine-tuning datasets; third-party model attestation policy in development.
LLM04 Model Denial of Service Computationally expensive prompts used to exhaust resources and degrade availability. Active Token budget enforcement, rate limiting per user/session, prompt length caps, anomaly detection.
LLM05 Supply Chain Vulnerabilities Compromised model weights, plugins, or third-party integrations introduced into the pipeline. Active Dependency pinning, SBOM generation, integrity verification for all model artefacts, PASS-rated MCP vetting.
LLM06 Sensitive Information Disclosure LLM inadvertently reveals PII, credentials, or confidential system context in responses. Active Memory vault encryption, sovereign data separation, no PII in system prompts, audit logging of outputs.
LLM07 Insecure Plugin Design Plugins or tool-use endpoints lacking proper auth, input validation, or scope controls. Active MCP PASS-rating framework, scoped tool permissions, user-consent gate before any tool execution.
LLM08 Excessive Agency LLM granted overly broad permissions enabling unintended autonomous actions. Active Minimal-capability defaults, explicit user authorisation for all agentic actions, human-in-the-loop checkpoints.
LLM09 Overreliance Users or systems acting on LLM output without appropriate verification, leading to harm. Active Confidence signalling in UI, explicit disclaimers for high-stakes domains (medical, legal, financial), source citation.
LLM10 Model Theft Extraction of proprietary model weights or architecture through adversarial querying. N/A MEOK does not train or host proprietary LLM weights. We use provider APIs with standard rate limiting and ToS enforcement.

Last reviewed: March 2026 · Based on OWASP LLM Top 10 v1.1

Bug Bounty

We reward responsible disclosure

MEOK.AI participates in coordinated vulnerability disclosure via huntr.com. Valid, in-scope reports are rewarded based on severity, impact, and quality of the submission.

  • Critical vulnerabilities: $10,000+ at our discretion
  • 90-day coordinated disclosure window before public release
  • CVE assignment coordination for qualifying vulnerabilities

Our disclosure commitment

We follow a structured 90-day coordinated disclosure timeline. Here's what to expect after you submit a report.

Day 1

Acknowledgement

We acknowledge receipt of your report within 24 hours and assign a tracking reference. You'll receive confirmation via the channel you submitted through.

Day 7

Triage & severity assessment

Our security team triages the report, assesses impact and severity, and communicates our initial findings to you. We'll confirm whether the report is in-scope and provide a preliminary severity rating.

Day 30

Remediation target

For critical and high-severity findings, we aim to deploy a fix within 30 days of confirmed triage. Complex issues may require extended timelines, which we'll communicate proactively.

Day 90

Coordinated public disclosure

At 90 days from triage, the vulnerability may be disclosed publicly — regardless of fix status. We'll work with you on the disclosure narrative and credit. Extension requests are considered case by case.

Contact

Report a vulnerability

Prefer to report directly? Email our security team. For sensitive reports, use our PGP key. We do not publicly disclose reports without your consent during the embargo window.

Security Team

Please include: affected component, reproduction steps, potential impact, and any proof-of-concept code. The more detail you provide, the faster we can triage and reward your finding.

Safe Harbour Statement

MEOK AI LTD supports responsible security research. If you conduct security research in good faith and in accordance with this policy, we will not initiate legal action against you under the Computer Misuse Act 1990 or equivalent legislation.

Good faith means: you make a genuine effort to avoid privacy violations, disruption to services, and data destruction; you access only the minimum data necessary to demonstrate the vulnerability; you report promptly and do not exploit findings beyond what is needed for proof of concept.

This safe harbour does not extend to activities that are clearly destructive, fraudulent, or involve the compromise of third-party systems or data not belonging to MEOK AI LTD.
