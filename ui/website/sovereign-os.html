What "local-first" actually means

Not "local until we need to sync." Not "local with some cloud features." Genuinely local-first: your conversations, memories, and model responses never leave your device unless you explicitly enable encrypted sync.

  • Conversations processed entirely on-device
  • Memory stored in encrypted local vector database
  • Optional cloud sync uses client-held keys — we can't read it
  • Works completely offline — no internet required
  • Delete data with cryptographic key erasure — instantly unrecoverable

Compliance built in, not bolted on

Privacy-by-design means the architecture itself is compliant — not a policy document added after the fact.

GDPR GDPR-K (Children) COPPA UK Online Safety Act California AB 2273 EU AI Act
  • Data residency: local-first, user-controlled
  • Encryption: client-held keys, zero-knowledge server
  • Deletion: cryptographic erasure (key destruction)
  • Audit: open-source client, reproducible builds
  • Third-party penetration testing (public reports)

Governance engine

The Sovereign OS includes a governance layer — not just privacy controls, but a system for how your AI makes decisions and what values it operates within.

  • Configurable value system — you define your companion's principles
  • Transparency log — see every decision the AI made and why
  • Override controls — always in the chair, never on autopilot
  • No engagement manipulation — no dark patterns in the AI itself
