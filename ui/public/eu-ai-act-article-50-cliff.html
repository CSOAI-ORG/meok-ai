EU AI Act Article 50 Cliff

All AI systems placed on the EU market must comply with Article 50 (transparency + watermarking) by 2 August 2026. Pre-existing general-purpose AI models have until 2 August 2026 (new) and 2 December 2026 (pre-existing). The Digital Omnibus delayed ONLY high-risk Annex III (to 2 Dec 2027) and Annex I product-embedded (to 2 Aug 2028). Article 50 is UNCHANGED.

What Article 50 requires:
  1. AI systems that interact with people must disclose they are AI (Article 50(1))
  2. Synthetic audio/image/video/text generators must mark outputs as artificially generated in a machine-readable way (Article 50(2))
  3. AI systems that generate or manipulate biometric data must mark them (Article 50(3))
  4. Providers of general-purpose AI models must put state-of-the-art watermarking on outputs (Article 50(4))
  5. Deployers of emotion recognition / biometric categorisation must inform subjects (Article 50(5))

Who must act

  • AI providers placing systems on the EU market — must build Article 50 compliance into the product
  • General-purpose AI model providers — must watermark all generated content
  • Deployers of biometric / emotion recognition — must inform subjects
  • Anyone selling AI products to EU users — even from outside the EU, the regulation applies
