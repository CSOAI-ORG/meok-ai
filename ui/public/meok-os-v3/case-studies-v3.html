12 researchers · 6 months · 3 continents

The challenge

The lab needed an AI platform that complied with their 13-framework bar (EU AI Act + GDPR + ISO 42001 + SOC 2 + HIPAA + ...), was MIT open source, and could be self-hosted on their EU sovereign compute. MEOK OS v3 + meok-keystone (the attestation API) + the sovereign data export worker. Every research interaction is Ed25519-signed, SOV3-anchored, and exportable. The BFT Council is open to the lab as a community voter.
