☁️ Sovereign Infrastructure

EU + US regions. OVHcloud, Hetzner, AWS GovCloud. Self-hostable. Air-gapped. Source-available for sovereign-tier.

🌍 The regions

🇪🇺 EU primary · eu-west-1 (Ireland)

OVHcloud + Hetzner. All EU customer data stays in EU. SCCs respected. EU-only compute available on request. 99.9% uptime.

  • OVHcloud: 1x SDD-128G (8 vCore, 128GB RAM, 2x 800GB SSD)
  • Hetzner: 1x CCX63 (48 vCore, 192GB RAM, 2x 1.92TB NVMe)
  • Network: 1Gbps OVHcloud + 1Gbps Hetzner + BGP failover

🇺🇸 US primary · us-east-1 (Virginia)

AWS GovCloud (FedRAMP Moderate). All US customer data stays in US. 99.9% uptime. SOC 2 in progress.

  • AWS GovCloud: 1x m5.4xlarge (16 vCPU, 64GB RAM, 1x 500GB EBS)
  • Network: 10Gbps AWS GovCloud + 1Gbps on-prem UK farm backup

🌍 CDN edge · global

Cloudflare + Vercel Edge. 200+ PoPs. TLS 1.3. Sovereign routing (data never leaves the region).

  • Cloudflare: 200+ PoPs, TLS 1.3, HTTP/3, Brotli
  • Vercel Edge: 18 PoPs, 50ms p50 latency, sovereign routing

🛡️ Sovereign compute

Sovereign compute means: your data never leaves your region or your jurisdiction. MEOK enforces this at the routing layer (Cloudflare Workers + Vercel Edge Middleware).

  • EU customers → EU-only compute (no US fallover)
  • US customers → US-only compute (no EU fallover)
  • Sovereign Custom → self-hosted on customer infrastructure
  • Self-hostable → your data center, your compute, your rules
