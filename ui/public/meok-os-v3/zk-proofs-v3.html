🛡️ What are ZK-proofs?

A zero-knowledge proof lets you prove a statement is true without revealing any information beyond the statement's truth. ZK-SNARK (succinct non-interactive argument of knowledge) is the most common type. ZK-STARK (scalable transparent argument of knowledge) is the more recent, post-quantum variant.

In MEOK: you can prove "I have a BFT voter seat" without revealing which one, "I'm over 18" without revealing your date of birth, or "I have 50K+ XP" without revealing your actual XP.

🔮 The 2 algorithms

🔐 ZK-SNARK (Groth16)

Succinct (200-byte proof). Fast verify (~5ms). Trusted setup required. Used in Zcash, Tornado Cash. Not quantum-safe.

Status: planned Q2 2027 · Quantum-safe: ✗

🔐 ZK-STARK (FRI)

Larger proofs (~50KB). Slower verify (~50ms). No trusted setup. Quantum-safe. Used in StarkNet, Polygon Miden.

Status: planned Q4 2027 · Quantum-safe: ✓
