💡 Why MIT?

MIT is the most permissive license. It means:

  • Use MEOK in commercial products (you can charge for it)
  • Modify MEOK freely (any modification)
  • Distribute MEOK freely (sell it, give it away)
  • Sublicense MEOK freely (MIT, Apache, GPL, BSD, your own license)
  • Use MEOK in proprietary software (closed-source, but the MEOK part stays MIT)

The only requirement is to keep the copyright notice + license text in any copy. That's it. The MIT promise is the sovereign promise.
