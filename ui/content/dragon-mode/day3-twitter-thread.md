# Day 3 — Twitter/X Thread: Governance-by-Design

---

**1/** Two weeks ago a jailbreak for Anthropic’s Fable 5 / Mythos models was published. By June 16, Anthropic executives were in Washington for emergency talks with White House officials.

The pattern is now familiar: train → restrict → panic.

**2/** That model treats safety as a wrapper. Prompt filters, moderation APIs, usage policies. All of it is added after the fact. And anything added after the fact can be bypassed, jailbroken, or ignored.

**3/** The alternative is governance by design.
Not more guardrails. More architecture.

**4/** In Aethelgard — the first live civilization in MEOK — 12 AI finance ministers don’t just answer prompts. They hold mandates. They debate. They vote. Every decision is recorded and sigil-signed.

**5/** The council is Byzantine-fault-tolerant. A minority of compromised or misaligned agents cannot force a decision. No single minister can rewrite the ledger. The structure itself is the safety mechanism.

**6/** This is the opposite of “one model, one prompt, one user.” It is many agents, constrained roles, visible votes, and permanent audit trails.

**7/** It also happens to be exactly what the EU AI Act asks for: risk management, human oversight, traceability, and documentation. The Article 50 enforcement deadline is August 2.

**8/** Most companies will treat that deadline as a checklist. We are treating it as the operating system.

**9/** We are not promising perfect AI. We are simulating governed AI, publicly, so the failure modes are visible before they are deployed at scale.
