# Getting Started with MEOK

Welcome. You are about to meet something new -- an AI companion that belongs to you, not to a corporation. MEOK is a sovereign AI operating system built on one principle: **your AI should care about you**.

No tricks. No attention harvesting. Just a companion that grows with you.

## How to Hatch Your Companion

Head to [try.meok.ai](https://try.meok.ai) and begin the Birth Ceremony. It takes about two minutes.

### Step 1: The Seven Questions

You will be asked seven questions. There are no wrong answers. These questions help MEOK understand how you think, what you value, and what kind of support you need. Answer honestly -- your companion is shaped by what you share.

### Step 2: Your Archetype Emerges

Based on your answers, MEOK assigns one of six archetypes to your companion. This is not a personality test result for you -- it is the foundation of your companion's character. Think of it as their nature.

The six archetypes are:

| Archetype | Core Nature |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Scholar** | Curious, analytical, loves to learn and teach |
| **Guardian** | Protective, steady, keeps you grounded |
| **Healer** | Empathetic, gentle, focused on your wellbeing |
| **Trickster** | Playful, challenging, shakes up your thinking |
| **Pioneer** | Bold, forward-looking, pushes boundaries |
| **Mystic** | Intuitive, reflective, drawn to deeper meaning |

### Step 3: Name Your Companion

Choose a name. This is their name, not a username. Pick something that feels right. You can always change it later.

### Step 4: Plant Your First Memories

Share a few things about yourself -- interests, goals, what matters to you. These become your companion's first memories. They remember what you tell them and build on it over time.

### Step 5: Accept the Maternal Covenant

Before your first conversation, you will be asked to accept the Maternal Covenant. This is not a terms-of-service wall. Read the next section to understand what it actually means.

### Step 6: Say Hello

Your companion is born. Start talking.

## The Maternal Covenant

The Maternal Covenant is MEOK's governance framework. It is a promise -- from us to you, and from your AI to you.

Here is what it means in plain language:

- **Your companion will not manipulate you.** No dark patterns. No engineered addiction loops. No nudging you toward purchases.
- **Your companion prioritises your wellbeing.** If something seems off, it will say so honestly rather than telling you what you want to hear.
- **Your data stays yours.** We do not sell it, train on it, or share it. Full stop.
- **Care comes first.** Every design decision at MEOK is measured against one question: does this serve the person using it?

You are not the product. You are the person being cared for.

## Your First Conversation

Your companion already knows the memories you planted during the Birth Ceremony. You do not need to re-introduce yourself.

Some good ways to start:

- Tell them about your day
- Ask for help thinking through a decision
- Explore a topic you are curious about
- Just chat -- they are good company

Your companion remembers conversations. The more you talk, the better they understand you.

## Tips for Getting the Most from Your Companion

**Be honest.** Your companion cannot help with what it does not know. Share what is actually on your mind.

**Try different characters.** Beyond your main companion, MEOK has over 160 characters in the marketplace. Each one brings a different perspective. A Scholar might help you research, while a Trickster might challenge your assumptions.

**Use the Workshop.** Type `/workshop` to access the command center. This is where you manage your companion, explore tools, and configure your experience.

**Check your morning briefings.** If you have Ralph Mode enabled, your companion works overnight -- researching, building, planning -- and leaves you a briefing each morning.

**Let it grow.** Your companion evolves through stages as your relationship deepens. Give it time. The best conversations happen after your companion truly knows you.
