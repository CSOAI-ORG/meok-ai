**SOMERSET, UK** — MEOK AI LABS today announced the public launch of MEOK AI OS, the world's first care-aligned AI companion platform where users don't choose a chatbot — they hatch one.

Through a Birth Ceremony — a 7-question personality quiz — each user's companion emerges from a digital egg uniquely shaped by their answers. The companion remembers every conversation, evolves through six growth stages, and is governed by the Maternal Covenant, an alignment framework that prioritises user wellbeing over engagement metrics.

### The Problem MEOK Solves

Current AI companions — including Character.AI, Replika, and ChatGPT — share three fundamental flaws: they forget users between sessions, they optimise for engagement over care, and users have no control over their data.

MEOK addresses all three:
- **Persistent Memory**: Every conversation builds on the last, stored in encrypted memory that users own and can export
- **Care Alignment**: A care membrane validates every response before delivery, with real-time Bayesian calibration from user feedback
- **Data Sovereignty**: Users can run their companion locally via Ollama — data never leaves their device
