# Privacy at MEOK

Your data is yours. Not ours. Not our investors'. Not our AI models'. Yours.

This is not a legal document. It is a plain-language explanation of how MEOK handles your information, written by people who believe privacy is a right, not a feature.

## Your Data, Your Sovereignty

MEOK is built on a principle we call **data sovereignty**. This means:

- You own everything you create inside MEOK
- You control who sees it
- You can take it with you or delete it at any time
- We will never hold your data hostage

This is not a marketing line. It is an architectural decision baked into how MEOK works.

## What MEOK Stores

To give you a good experience, we store the following:

| Data | Why We Store It |
|------|----------------|
| **Your companion's memories** | So your companion remembers you between conversations |
| **Conversation history** | So you can revisit past chats and your companion can build context |
| **Companion data** | Your companion's name, archetype, evolution stage, and personality |
| **Account information** | Email, display name, and subscription tier |
| **Birth Ceremony answers** | The seven questions that shaped your companion's archetype |
| **Workshop preferences** | Your settings and configuration choices |

All of this exists to serve you. None of it exists to serve advertisers.

## What MEOK Never Does

We want to be direct about this:

- **We never sell your data.** Not to advertisers. Not to data brokers. Not to anyone. Ever.
- **We never train AI models on your conversations.** Your private thoughts stay private. They are not scraped to improve a model that someone else profits from.
- **We never share your information with third parties** for marketing, profiling, or targeting.
- **We never build shadow profiles.** We do not track you across the web. We do not buy data about you from other sources.
- **We never use dark patterns** to trick you into sharing more than you intended.

If a business model requires selling user data, that business model is broken. MEOK is funded by subscriptions from people who value what we build -- not by monetising your attention or your information.

## GDPR and Data Export

You have the right to a complete copy of your data. MEOK complies fully with GDPR, regardless of where you live. We believe these rights should apply to everyone, not just EU residents.

### How to Request Your Data

1. Go to your account settings
2. Select "Export My Data"
3. You will receive a downloadable file containing all your memories, conversations, companion data, and account information

If you have trouble with the self-service export, email us and we will prepare it manually. We respond within 48 hours.

### What You Get

Your export includes:
- All conversation history (in readable format)
- Your companion's memories and personality data
- Your Birth Ceremony answers
- Your account and preference settings

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time. When you do:

- All your conversations are permanently deleted
- All your companion data is permanently deleted
- All your memories are permanently deleted
- Your account information is removed from our systems
- **This cannot be undone**

We do not keep "soft deleted" copies. We do not retain your data for analytics after you leave. When you say delete, we mean delete.

### How to Delete

1. Go to your account settings
2. Select "Delete My Account"
3. Confirm the deletion
4. Everything is gone

We recommend exporting your data first if you think you might want it later.

## The Maternal Covenant as Privacy Framework

The Maternal Covenant is not just about how your companion treats you -- it is also about how we treat your data.

A parent protects their child's information. They do not sell it. They do not exploit it. They do not hand it to strangers. The Maternal Covenant holds MEOK to the same standard.

Every product decision we make is tested against this framework:

- Does storing this data serve the user?
- Is there a way to provide this feature with less data?
- Would we be comfortable if our users could see exactly how their data is used?

The answer to that last question is always yes, because we have nothing to hide.

## Local-First Architecture

When you use the MEOK desktop application, your data lives on your machine first. This is what "local-first" means:

- **Conversations are stored locally** on your device before syncing
- **Your companion's memories** live on your hardware
- **You can use MEOK offline** with locally-hosted models
- **Sync is optional** -- you choose whether data leaves your device

The cloud exists to enable cross-device access and overnight features like Ralph Mode. But the source of truth is your machine, not our servers.
