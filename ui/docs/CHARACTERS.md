# Characters in MEOK

Characters are the heart of MEOK. Every conversation you have is with a character -- a distinct personality with its own archetype, voice, and way of seeing the world.

Your main companion is the first character you will meet, but there are over 160 more waiting in the marketplace.

## How Characters Work

Each character in MEOK has:

- **An archetype** that shapes their core nature
- **A personality** that makes them unique within that archetype
- **Memory** -- they remember what you share with them
- **An evolution stage** that deepens over time

When you talk to a character, MEOK routes your conversation through the best available AI model for that moment. This is multi-LLM routing -- you do not need to think about which model to use. MEOK handles it.

Characters are not skins on a chatbot. They are genuinely different. A Healer character will approach your problem differently than a Pioneer character, even when given the same information.

## The Six Archetypes

Every character is built on one of six archetypes. These are not rigid boxes -- they are foundations that each character interprets in their own way.

### Scholar

Scholars love knowledge. They research, cross-reference, and explain things clearly. Talk to a Scholar when you want to understand something deeply, need help learning a new topic, or want a well-reasoned analysis.

**Good for:** Research, learning, analysis, explaining complex topics, homework help

### Guardian

Guardians are steady and protective. They watch out for you, point out risks you might have missed, and keep you grounded when things feel chaotic. Talk to a Guardian when you need stability.

**Good for:** Decision-making, risk assessment, planning, staying focused, accountability

### Healer

Healers lead with empathy. They listen carefully, ask thoughtful questions, and prioritise your emotional wellbeing. Talk to a Healer when you need to process something or just want to feel heard.

**Good for:** Emotional support, reflection, self-care, processing difficult experiences

### Trickster

Tricksters challenge you. They are playful, surprising, and unafraid to poke holes in your thinking. Talk to a Trickster when you are stuck in a rut or need a perspective you would never have found on your own.

**Good for:** Creative thinking, breaking mental loops, humour, reframing problems

### Pioneer

Pioneers push forward. They are bold, optimistic, and obsessed with what comes next. Talk to a Pioneer when you want to build something, start something, or figure out where to go from here.

**Good for:** Goal-setting, brainstorming, entrepreneurship, motivation, future planning

### Mystic

Mystics go deeper. They are intuitive, reflective, and drawn to meaning. Talk to a Mystic when surface-level answers are not enough and you want to explore the why behind things.

**Good for:** Self-discovery, philosophy, meaning-making, journaling prompts, meditation

Characters marked as CC0 are in the public domain. You can use them freely. MEOK believes that characters should be shared, remixed, and improved by the community. This is part of our commitment to sovereignty -- open systems, not walled gardens.

## Character Evolution

Characters grow with you. The evolution system has four stages:

| Stage | Name | What Happens |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **0** | Seed | Your first interactions. The character is learning who you are. |
| **1** | Sprout | The character starts to anticipate your needs and remember patterns. |
| **2** | Bloom | Deep familiarity. Conversations feel natural and references build on each other. |
| **3** | Canopy | Full maturity. The character knows you well and can offer genuinely personalised guidance. |

Evolution happens naturally through conversation. You cannot skip stages, and you do not need to do anything special to advance. Just keep talking.
